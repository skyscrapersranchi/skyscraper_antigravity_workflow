## 1. BRAND POSITIONING

Skyscraper should feel like a **digital architectural practice**, not a property portal. The site earns "premium" through composition, restraint, precision and interaction — never through decorative effects, gradients, or generic real-estate templates.

**Locked visual identity:**
- Skyscraper logo: circular gray/gold "S" mark + "SKYSCRAPER" wordmark + "Bring home to quality" script tagline (use the supplied logo files exactly — light and dark versions provided; never redraw)
- Palette family: architectural black, warm paper/stone tones, charcoal, with the gold/gray brand accent used sparingly
- Typography: editorial serif (headlines) + precise sans (body/UI) — do not mix in more than these two families
- Large negative space over dense grids

**Hard AVOID list (do not build any of this):**
- Generic real-estate hero cards
- Fake statistics or invented numbers
- Excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism, gold-luxury gradients
- Floating particles, cursor gimmicks, random bounce animations
- "SALE" / "BOOK NOW" banner language anywhere
- Property-portal-style spec boxes
- Fake lifestyle stock photography
- Any invented project, amenity, award, price, RERA number, or address

## 3. HERO VIDEO (SECTION 1)

The ~1:10 cinematic landing film is already produced. The builder frames it; it does not modify it.

- Desktop: full-bleed, 16:9 source preserved, hero stage may expand vertically but framing stays cinematic. Keep door/person/action in the central safe region.
- Mobile: portrait stage with a deliberate center-weighted crop protecting faces, door, and primary action. Do NOT commission or fabricate a separate mobile video — reuse the same asset with a smart crop, or fall back to a contained 16:9 panel on a dark background if cropping damages the story.
- **No text, no logo, no CTA burned into or laid over the film during playback.** No cookie banner, location prompt, enquiry form, or popup over the cinematic opening.
- Sequence: (1) black/quiet entry, no headline → (2) door/arrival, film plays uninterrupted → (3) life sequence (child → window → couple → generations), preserved as delivered → (4) brand resolution (SKYSCRAPER / Bring home to quality) → (5) handoff — black/logo resolves into the first website section.
- Builder may add: mute/unmute control, replay control, subtle loading state. Nothing else.

## 4. 35-SECOND ARCHITECTURAL SCROLL (SECTION 5)

Supplied as prepared image frames (already produced) — treat as a scroll-driven image film, FPV-style movement through real architectural space. **No text is baked into any frame.**

| Phase | Scroll window | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 0–5s | Exterior / approach / first reveal |
| Arrival | 5–10s | Entrance / threshold |
| Transition | 10–17s | Architecture becomes the subject |
| Interior | 17–25s | Interior work / spatial progression |
| Detail | 25–31s | Material / finish detail |
| Resolution | 31–35s | Final composition → hand off to project story |

**Implementation logic:**
- Input: scroll position → Output: frame index. Movement must feel continuous, not slideshow-like.
- Pin the visual while the section consumes scroll height; preload initial frames, progressively load the rest.
- Desktop: large visual field, text in a separate editorial column. Mobile: image dominates, short text between visual beats.
- Reduced-motion: offer a static fallback presentation.
- Text layer is external and left open to decide after seeing frames in-browser — short editorial words genuinely supported by the scene (architecture, material, light, space, craft, progress). Do not permanently lock copy to frames before testing.
