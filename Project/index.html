Architecture · Spatial Practice

We craft spaces that frame human calm.

Skyscraper operates as a digital architectural practice grounded in Jharkhand. We reject the hurried conventions of mass property development — the loud billboards, disposable materials, and generic floorplates.

Our work is defined by structural permanence, natural light orientation, generous negative space, and uncompromised material honesty. Every threshold is considered. Every square foot is shaped to outlive fashion.

The Skyscraper Philosophy

Jharkhand · Ranchi & Hazaribagh

Permanence over novelty.

In a rapidly developing region, we build residences engineered for calm. We align our structural engineering with certified RCC earthquake-resistant frameworks, high-specification internal wiring, and monolithic stone finishes.

This is not real estate speculation. It is a commitment to creating enduring family sanctuaries where memories take root across decades.

Principle 01

SPACE

Room to breathe. High internal clear heights, generous room dimensions, and uncompromised separation between private sleeping suites and shared living expanses.

Principle 02

LIGHT

Orientation designed around the sun's trajectory. Wide aluminium sliding windows invite morning illumination while natural cross-ventilation keeps interiors temperate and fresh.

Principle 03

MATERIAL

Honest materiality that matures with age. Monolithic granite countertops, 2'x2' vitrified tile floors, marble stairwells, and weatherproof exterior coatings.

Principle 04

CRAFT

Structural rigor beneath the surface. Certified RCC frame structural design, multicore copper wiring, automated silent power backup, and centralized water storage per block.

Heritage & Sustainability · 4 Decades of Excellence

Building for generations, not seasons.

"Bring home to quality" — Crafting Spaces, Creating Memories.

For over four decades, M/S Skyscraper has stood as a vanguard of architectural permanence across Jharkhand. Our practice across Ranchi and Hazaribagh is anchored in an unyielding conviction: every home must be a living sanctuary that enriches both family life and its natural habitat.

Sustainable development is not a superficial addition to our practice; it is our foundational baseline. We combine bioclimatic spatial planning with precision structural engineering, ensuring minimal resource consumption, natural thermal comfort, and responsible ecological stewardship.

"True luxury resides in tranquility — breathing clean air, basking in natural dawn illumination, and knowing your home was engineered to protect the land it rests upon."

Technical Document · Verbatim Specification

Engineering & Material System

Spec 01

Structure

RCC frame, earthquake-resistant design engineered in compliance with strict structural stability codes.

Spec 02

Doors & Windows

Flush main door with central lock; internal flush doors with SS hardware, wooden/marble frames; aluminium sliding windows with glass panels.

Spec 03

Flooring

Vitrified 2'x2' tiles (covered flat area); marble in corridors/stairs; PCC + checkered tiles in parking; paver blocks in open spaces.

Spec 04

Bathroom

Anti-skid vitrified floor tiles; glazed wall tiles to 7ft height for enduring moisture resistance and effortless maintenance.

Spec 05

Kitchen

Granite platform with steel sink; glazed tiles 2ft above platform; hot/cold water provision in sink.

Spec 06

Lift

6-passenger capacity, standard make passenger elevator serving all residential floors.

Spec 07

Walls

Internal cement + POP finish; external weatherproof textured paint engineered to withstand seasonal monsoon cycles.

Spec 08

CCTV Surveillance

CCTV surveillance provided as a standard fixed-charge facility across the property, alongside intercom and lift access — per brochure specifications.

Spec 09

Water Supply

Common water tank per block ensuring continuous pressure and uninterrupted residential supply.

Spec 10

Generator Backup

Silent mode generator powering common lighting, lift, pump + 400W emergency power dedicated per flat.

Spec 11

Electrical

Copper multi-core wiring throughout; room/kitchen/bathroom points per brochure specifications.

Spec 12

Puja Room

A dedicated puja room is included within the residential layout, positioned as a quiet, separate space within the home — per the approved floor plans.

Associated Provisions

Silent generator backup · Intercom integration · CCTV perimeter security · Facility & Lift maintenance · Reserved car & two-wheeler parking.
